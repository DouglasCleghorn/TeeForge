Status: completed on 2026-08-22.

Source state: the initial signed repository commit containing this record. The

## TeeStream results

Each operation creates two `MemoryStream` destinations and writes the payload to
both. Allocations therefore include both payload copies and stream objects.

| Payload | Implementation | Mean | Allocated |
| ---: | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 4 KiB | Manual sequential loop | 381.4 ns | 8,368 B |
| 4 KiB | `TeeStream` sequential | 669.1 ns | 8,712 B |
| 64 KiB | Manual sequential loop | 5.969 us | 131,248 B |
| 64 KiB | `TeeStream` sequential | 5.693 us | 131,596 B |
| 1 MiB | Manual sequential loop | 321.029 us | 2,097,510 B |
| 1 MiB | `TeeStream` sequential | 605.327 us | 2,101,745 B |

The wrapper adds roughly 344--4,235 B per operation in this deliberately
allocation-heavy construction benchmark. The 64 KiB timings overlap; 4 KiB
shows fixed dispatch overhead, while the noisy 1 MiB result warrants profiling
before making performance claims about large synchronous writes.

This construction-inclusive test meets the provisional 10% target only at
64 KiB. A separate steady-state experiment measures the write path without
repeated construction and buffer allocation.

## TeePipe results

| Payload | Implementation | Readers | Mean | Allocated |
| ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 4 KiB | Microsoft `Pipe` | 1 | 1.392 us | 880 B |
| 4 KiB | `TeePipe` | 1 | 1.618 us | 1,504 B |
| 4 KiB | Four Microsoft `Pipe` instances | 4 | 4.801 us | 3,793 B |
| 4 KiB | One `TeePipe` | 4 | 3.573 us | 3,158 B |
| 64 KiB | Microsoft `Pipe` | 1 | 7.219 us | 2,560 B |
| 64 KiB | `TeePipe` | 1 | 7.881 us | 3,432 B |
| 64 KiB | Four Microsoft `Pipe` instances | 4 | 23.281 us | 11,000 B |
| 64 KiB | One `TeePipe` | 4 | 11.981 us | 5,177 B |
| 1 MiB | Microsoft `Pipe` | 1 | 88.168 us | 29,600 B |
| 1 MiB | `TeePipe` | 1 | 92.959 us | 30,472 B |
| 1 MiB | Four Microsoft `Pipe` instances | 4 | 276.298 us | 118,966 B |
| 1 MiB | One `TeePipe` | 4 | 100.810 us | 32,217 B |

Single-reader `TeePipe` overhead falls from about 16% at 4 KiB to about 5% at
1 MiB. With four readers, shared payload storage is the intended win: at 1 MiB,
`TeePipe` took about 36% of the time and 27% of the managed allocation of four
independent Pipes. Treat these results as a baseline from one machine, not as a
cross-machine guarantee.

The 4 KiB single-reader result misses the provisional 15% target by roughly one
percentage point; the 64 KiB and 1 MiB cases meet it. All multi-reader cases
materially outperform the equivalent independent-Pipe broadcast.

## Follow-up

- Profile the 1 MiB synchronous `TeeStream` case.
- Add realistic file and network destinations without conflating device latency
  with wrapper overhead.
- Track these cases across releases; do not compare results collected under
  different runtime, power, or hardware configurations as if they were paired.
